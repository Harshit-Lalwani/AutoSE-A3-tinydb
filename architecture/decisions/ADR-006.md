# ADR-006: MiniMax M3.1 Flash for the builders, Nemotron-3 Ultra for the reviewer, free tiers only

**Date:** 2026-11-24 · **Status:** Superseded by [ADR-011](ADR-011.md) · **Decision area:** D6 Models

## Context
Constraints measured on 2026-11-24: OpenRouter key is free tier (no paid models); Groq free tier
allows 8,000 tokens per minute, too little for an agent loop; NVIDIA NIM free tier serves MiniMax
M3.1 Flash (tool call answered in 1.8 s) and Nemotron-3 Ultra 550B (5.5 s); Kimi K3.1 Pro and
GLM-5.3 Flash on NIM did not answer within 200 s. We want the reviewer from a different model
family than the builders, so their mistakes are less correlated.

## Decision
| Role | Primary | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Spec, design, coder, tester | `minimaxai/minimax-m3.1-flash` (NIM) | `google/gemma-4-31b-it:free` (OpenRouter) |
| Reviewer | `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b` (NIM) | same model, `:free` on OpenRouter |

The fallback is used only after two failed calls on the primary. The orchestrator logs the model
actually used for each call; if a builder ever ran on the reviewer's model family it is reported.

## Alternatives considered
- **One model everywhere.** Simpler; but a reviewer that shares the builder's blind spots is the
  failure we most want to avoid (tested in E1).
- **Stronger paid model (e.g. Claude Sonnet 5.5) for the coder.** Likely higher pass rate, but needs
  a paid key and spend; deferred until free models prove too weak.
- **Groq gpt-oss-120b as reviewer.** Fast and a third family, but 8k TPM cannot hold a repo diff plus
  test output in an agent loop.

## Consequences
- Zero model cost; speed limited by NIM free rate limits (about 40 requests per minute).
- The builder model is a "flash" model: expect more retries; budgets (ADR-009) matter.
